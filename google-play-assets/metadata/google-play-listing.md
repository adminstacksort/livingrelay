# LivingRelay Google Play Listing

## App Name

LivingRelay

## Short Description

AI maintenance management for rental repairs over SMS.

## Full Description

LivingRelay helps small property teams coordinate rental repairs without losing context in scattered text threads.

Tenants can report maintenance issues from their phone with unit details, symptoms, photos, and access notes. Managers can review AI-assisted triage, approve next steps, coordinate vendors, and keep owners informed. Owners can review repair context, approve estimates, and track invoice records. Vendors can see assigned jobs, accept or decline requests, and keep repair updates tied to the property record.

LivingRelay is built for rental maintenance workflows where communication, approvals, vendor coordination, and invoice history all need to stay connected.

Key features:

- Phone and PIN access with SMS verification
- Property setup for managers and owner operators
- Tenant repair intake with issue, unit, access, and photo fields
- AI-assisted trade, urgency, and estimate triage
- Manager and owner approval workflow
- Vendor coordination and job status actions
- Invoice records and off-platform payment status tracking
- Property-level repair history for better owner visibility

Repair payments are not processed in LivingRelay. The app keeps invoice records and payment status for repairs paid directly between owners, managers, and vendors.

LivingRelay is intended for property managers, rental owners, tenants, and service vendors who need a simple shared workflow for maintenance requests.
